## Q&A

* changes to key personnel/subaward sites: yes
* project manager is a reasonable cost. all costs need to meet standards of
  CFRXX and be in compliance with university
* industry partner funds for staff for training students. yes.
* industry salary/stipend: option, but assumption with design is to give as much
  support to the university to support the student as possible. with a
  transition, there are a lot of places for delay to setup support for the
  student. continuous funding for the student through the university. if
  industry wants to fund the student, we are all for it though.
* cohort of 20 students in year 1, or a new cohort each year? (deferred to
  Sharon). having students start in "year 1". if cohort brought all the way
  through the award, that is a successful pilot.
* first cohort starts within first year. first year is to give universities a
  year to recruit and to create research plans for the cohort. start of cohort
  would be Fall 2028.
* number of secondary challenge areas: absolutley, yes (to ask if more than one)
* are letters of collab allowed? allowed on page 18 of NOFO. not letters of rec.
* what SP: leaders of the training program, or all potential mentors at the
  institution? (Sharon) have seen LOIs that have done both, so will accept
  without predjudice. however, this is a heavy lift, so having institutional
  leadership as part of the PI team is important. so, signifies that the whole
  instituion is supportive of that effort. we did not intend for every single
  mentor to be named, as there is that one-year lead up time. you may not know
  all of the mentors when LOI is received.
* two cohorts instead of one? (absolutely, yes, they can have two cohorts).
  however, can they continue supporting the student outside of the five years?
  they have a NCE that would allow that in a DOE award, but keep in mind that
  the purpose of the pilot is to show that we can do this.
* faculty summer salary for mentoring/supervising: yes. if they are doing so,
  and if such costs are reasonable, allowable, allocable, and necessary.
* balance strnegh of fellowship design and alignment with genesis mission: which
  is a higher priority? (sharon) you need to do both in order to be successful 
* budget must adhere to cost principals oof 2 CRF 200 and be consistent with
  institutional policies and procedures. questions getting this response
  included:
    - staff effort dedicated to directly supporting core project functions
      (workforce development, coordination, workshop planning, etc) 
    - programmatic support staff for cohort related activities that enrich the
      academic and research experience allowable in the bduget (eg, academic
      program manager)
    - dedicated project manager/coordinator. limits on the level of effort?
    - travel for mentors to travel on behalf of thesis progress for the student.
    - curriculum development, like a course buy-out
    - industry partner time
* recruitment in costs: if necessary, yes. if not, no.
* institutions wiht campuses in several locations.
* the intent is for the application to support your instittion's program.
* We expect LOI will be somewhat uncertain, and that will depend on students
  recruited and their interests.
* compute hours: an allowable expense.
* should preference for students with MS program (up to the instittion, what
  makes sense)
* no pref for institution-wide versus one focus area
* lab partners, not required, but useful if known.
* cohort starting: we understand that cohorts start with the academci year, so
  the intent is to give the people a year to get started. can be plus/minus a
  little bit of time once we get the award dates finalized.
* how to handle people already in PhD programs. the individual would have a time
  period (4 years) to finish the fellowship PhD, irrespective if they are in a
  PhD program already. the four years begins at the start of the fellowship, as
  the fellwship might have different criteria than the person's previous PhD
  fellowship.
* LOI is a fiarly high-level description of what you intend to do. guesses,
  estimates are all appropriate. also note that PIs do not submit the LOI, the
  university does.
* industry = a for-profit organization 
* two students with same PI is allowable.
* intent of pilot: show that a domestic student can get a PhD in four years.
  show that four-year PHDs are possible.
* penalty or problem if some students do not graduate in that four-year
  timeline? life happens.
